Introduction

Hi, I'm Alexander.

I'm a Mechanical Engineering student at Fontys University of Applied Sciences in Eindhoven. Engineering is not just what I study, it's how I think. I genuinely enjoy pulling something apart to understand it, then figuring out how to make it better. That goes for mechanical systems, but also for software, tools and anything new that crosses my path. New technology excites me, and I try to stay close to what's developing.

Outside of my studies I work in marketing and content creation, which taught me that communicating an idea clearly is just as important as having one. It also pushed me to write and think about audiences, stories and how information lands.

I follow geopolitics closely. The way countries, economies and power structures interact fascinates me, and I think understanding the world at that level makes you better at almost everything else.

Travel is a big part of who I am. I explore Europe mostly by train, and I'm happiest when there's a mountain, a coastline or somewhere genuinely remote nearby. Nature recharges me in a way little else does, and most of my best experiences have happened far from a screen.

This site is a mix of CV and personal diary. You'll find projects I've worked on, experiences that shaped me, and things I've picked up along the way. Feel free to look around.
