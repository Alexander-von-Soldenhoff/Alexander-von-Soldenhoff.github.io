My Story

I was born in Nijmegen, in the Netherlands, but grew up in Spain from the age of three. My father is German, my background is mixed, and Spanish became the language I think in. That combination of cultures has always been part of how I see things: I don't fully belong anywhere in particular, which sounds like a problem but is actually quite useful.

Growing up on the Costa del Sol meant sun, mountains and the sea within reach at the same time. I spent a lot of time outside, hiking, travelling, and paying attention to how places work and what makes them different from one another. That curiosity about the world around me never really switched off. It just expanded into other directions over time, including geopolitics, technology and how systems, whether mechanical or political, hold together or fall apart.

For my final two years of secondary school I completed both the International Baccalaureate and the Spanish Bachillerato at the same time. It was a heavy workload but it gave me something I didn't expect: a real ability to manage complexity and keep moving when things are demanding. The IB in particular pushed me towards the sciences, and by the end of it I knew I wanted to build things, not just study them.

In early 2026 I moved back to the Netherlands to start Mechanical Engineering at Fontys in Eindhoven. Moving back to a country I was born in but didn't grow up in is a strange experience. The language came back quickly. The weather did not improve. But Eindhoven is a good city for someone interested in technology and design, and the programme is exactly what I was looking for: applied, hands-on and focused on real problems from day one.

Outside of my studies I work in marketing and content creation, I follow geopolitics closely, and I travel as much as I can, mostly by train through Europe. I'm most at ease when I'm somewhere new, figuring out how it works.
